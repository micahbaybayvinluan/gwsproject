Everyone

Signing in

  1. Type your own username (never share it).
  2. Type your password. The first time, you choose a new one and accept the accountability statement.
  3. Press Sign in.

The dashboard and the menusigned in as sales.westave

  1. The menu on the left lists only the screens of your role. Each screen tells at the top what it is for.
  2. The bell shows new notifications (approvals, reminders, decisions). Click one to open the document.
  3. The dashboard shows what needs you today, for example today's sales by payment and the cash on hand.

Help & Guidesigned in as sales.westave

  1. Open Help & Guide from the menu.
  2. My guide has the step-by-step guide for your own role.
  3. Type a question in the Ask box.

Sales Associate

Recording a salesigned in as sales.westave

  1. Type the DR / SI number from the paper receipt.
  2. Choose the channel (walk-in, delivery, dealer…).
  3. Choose the payment mode: cash, online, credit card or on credit (AR / PDC).
  4. Type the product name or scan the barcode, then the quantity.
  5. Press Save. Stock is deducted at once.

Selling on credit (AR) and PDCsigned in as sales.westave

  1. Choose what the customer is: Dealer, Franchisee, Agent or Other customer.
  2. Only that kind of customer is listed. Pick the customer.
  3. Type the due date.
  4. Tick There is a PDC only when the customer gave a post-dated cheque.
  5. Then type the bank, cheque number and cheque date.

Expenses paid from the cash on handsigned in as sales.westave

  1. Choose the expense account (for example Meralco).
  2. Type the amount and the payee.
  3. Choose Cash on hand (sales cash not yet deposited) or Cash fund.
  4. The screen shows how much cash on hand the branch has.
  5. Press Save expense, then attach the receipt.

Not enough cash on handsigned in as sales.westave

  1. If the expense is more than the cash on hand, this notice pops up and nothing is saved.
  2. Pay it from the cash fund instead, or ask Accounting to pay it from the bank.

Receiving stock from the warehousesigned in as sales.westave

  1. If everything arrived complete, tick Tick all.
  2. Otherwise tick each line that arrived complete…
  3. …and for a line that did not, type the quantity you actually got and what is wrong.
  4. An item that arrived but is not on the form: press this and add it.
  5. Press Confirm receipt. A difference goes to the Head Auditor automatically.

Following a difference on a transfersigned in as sales.westave

  1. The transfer page shows the difference: on the form, received, short or extra.
  2. The steps: Head Auditor → sending branch (2 days) → Owner if they disagree.
  3. When settled, the -002 adjustment form returns the missing items to the sender.

Collecting a customer payment (AR)signed in as sales.westave

  1. Tick the invoice(s) of the customer who paid.
  2. Type the amount received.
  3. Choose how it was paid. For online and card payments choose the account and upload the proof.
  4. Send it. Accounting approves it before the balance goes down.

Submitting the Daily Sales Reportsigned in as sales.westave

  1. At the end of the day, check the report and press Submit today's report. Tick "true and correct" and confirm. The day then closes.

Opening the document behind a stock movementsigned in as sales.westave

  1. In Inventory Reports, the stock ledger lists every movement.
  2. Click the document (Sale DR, Pull-out, Transfer-in…) to open it.

Warehouse Associate

Receiving a supplier deliverysigned in as wh.assoc

  1. Choose the supplier (by code).
  2. Type the supplier's invoice / DR number.
  3. Upload Supplier Delivery Receipt: a photo or PDF of the supplier's DR.
  4. Add each product with quantity, freebies, expiry and batch.
  5. Press Create draft, check it and submit. The In-Charge checks the goods first, then the Head Auditor approves the cost; the stock is then added.

Warehouse In-Charge

Answering a difference your warehouse sentsigned in as wh.incharge

  1. The receiving branch got less than your form, and the Head Auditor confirmed it.
  2. Check your shelves and packing and type what you found.
  3. Agree: the -002 adjustment form is made automatically.
  4. Disagree: the Owner decides. Answer within 2 days.

My Approvalssigned in as wh.incharge

  1. Everything waiting for you is in My Approvals: your associates' goods in and out, e-commerce pull-outs and transfer differences.
  2. Click a line to see the details, then decide. A transfer difference your warehouse sent shows Agree / Disagree (a reason is required to disagree).

Head Auditor

Reviewing a transfer differencesigned in as head.auditor

  1. The table shows what was on the form, what was received and the note.
  2. Confirm the difference: it goes to the sending branch to agree.
  3. Receiver miscounted: the receiving branch gets the items as on the form.

Cash on hand of every branchsigned in as head.auditor

  1. See each branch's undeposited cash and deadlines, and set the days allowed to deposit.

Accounting Head

Approving an AR paymentsigned in as acct.head

  1. See the account it was deposited to, the amount and how it was paid.
  2. The proof of payment is right there; click it to enlarge.
  3. The invoices being paid.
  4. Approve when the money is in the account.
  5. Reject asks for the reason.

Rejecting with a reasonsigned in as acct.head

  1. Choose the reason: wrong amount, wrong proof, proof unclear, money not received, wrong account, wrong customer or invoice, duplicate, wrong date, cheque bounced, or other.
  2. Add details if needed.
  3. Press Reject payment. The branch sees the reason.

HR Staff

Transfer differences in HR Noticessigned in as hr.staff

  1. Every confirmed transfer difference comes here with the person answerable.
  2. The number of cases in 30 days; from the third it is marked in red.
  3. Refer to the Owner for a more serious consideration.

Sales Manager

Setting and following sales targetssigned in as sales.manager

  1. Each branch (and e-commerce platform) with its target and sales so far.
  2. The bar shows % achieved; the dark line is how much of the month has gone. Green = on track.
  3. To set a target: choose the branch or agent and type the amount…
  4. …and send it. The Owner approves it.

Agent

My Salessigned in as agent.jerick

  1. Your target for the month.
  2. How much of it is done (green = on track).
  3. Your customers who have not paid yet, nearest due first.
  4. Your sales at every branch.

Owner (Admin)

Approving targets and other requestssigned in as admin

  1. Requests are grouped by type, for example the Sales Manager's targets.
  2. Approve (or reject) one…
  3. …or tick several and use Approve selected.

Creating a user accountsigned in as admin

  1. In Users & Roles, create one account per person: username, full name, company ID, role and branch. Press Generate for a temporary password.
