# Restocking: what is built and what we recommend next

## Built (see USER-GUIDE → Restock & Purchase Orders, decisions D135–D139)
Generate per supplier → days left per branch → suggested transfer from existing stock first → suggested order → edit by hand → Owner approval → branches told → three printed copies → delivery linked to the order.

## Recommended next (each is small and fits the same screen)
1. **Lead time per supplier.** Order when stock will run out *before the supplier can deliver* (days left < lead time + safety days), and show "order by" dates.
2. **Overdue orders.** Alert the Head Auditor and Owner when a Sent order passes its expected date; show a fill-rate scorecard per supplier (ordered vs received, on time).
3. **Ordered vs received differences.** A short delivery or an extra item opens a note to the Head Auditor, as transfer differences do.
4. **Promo- and season-aware averages.** Raise the average per day when a promo is coming (the promo calendar already exists) and ignore days with no stock.
5. **Weekly draft.** A weekly job drafts the request for the Head Auditor to review (never sends by itself).
6. **Near-expiry first.** Suggest transfers from the batch nearest to expiry, and list slow or dead stock to move or promote before reordering.
7. **Cash and budget check.** Show the order's value (cost hidden from branches) against the cash and what is due to suppliers, before the Owner approves.
8. **Small-order rule.** Orders under an amount the Owner sets are approved by the Head Auditor; larger ones by the Owner.
9. **Payment schedule.** Use the supplier's terms (days) to show when each order will fall due, linked to the AP ageing.
10. **Prefilled pull-out.** A button on Restock Plan that opens a pull-out form already filled with the branch's quantities.
