# Wheysted members: more ways to learn about customers and boost sales

> **Status (2026-10-06): all 23 ideas below are built.** See the *Wheysted member program* section of the User Guide and decisions D122–D128. Messenger, Viber and WhatsApp are click-to-chat lists for the staff (no paid sending service); automatic SMS / email need SMTP / Semaphore set up; automatic messages are off until the Owner switches them on (back-in-stock is on).


Recommended in this order (the first group needs almost no extra work from staff).

## 1. Use what we already collect (next to build)
1. **Re-order reminder per member** (we already compute how long a product lasts): an automatic SMS / email "your Prothin should be about done, same again?" with the member's favorite flavor. Biggest repeat-sales lever.
2. **Win-back campaigns** for the At risk and Lapsed segments: one tick, a message with a small voucher, tracked to see who came back.
3. **Birthday month treat**: automatic message with a discount code to the Birthday segment.
4. **Campaign results**: for every campaign, count the members who bought within 7 days afterwards and the amount (campaign → sales).
5. **Cross-sell suggestions**: "members who buy whey also buy creatine / shaker": at the counter, show the member's favorite and the usual add-on.
6. **Branch and time heat-map**: which branch and which hours / weekdays each segment buys, to staff and promote better.

## 2. Reward and tier programs
7. **Points / tiers (Bronze, Silver, Gold)** from the amount spent in 12 months, shown on the QR card and the member page; the existing 6-Pack card can become one of the rewards.
8. **Vouchers and promo codes** tied to a member (single use, expiry), redeemed by scanning the QR; track redemption and margin.
9. **Referral rewards**: a member refers a friend with their member number; both get a reward after the friend's first purchase.
10. **Birthday and anniversary** dates (we already keep the birthday).

## 3. Learn more about each customer (ask once, use forever)
11. **Fitness profile on the member page**: goal (muscle gain, weight loss, endurance), gym or none, training days, budget range, dietary needs (lactose-free, vegan), flavor likes / dislikes. Staff use it to recommend; segments can use it.
12. **How did you hear about us** (Facebook, TikTok, gym, friend, walk-in): which channels bring members, and which bring the best ones.
13. **Gym / outlet link**: tag a member to the agent outlet (gym) they train at, so the agents see their members and the Sales Manager sees sales by gym.
14. **Preferred contact** (SMS, email, Messenger) and best time to message.

## 4. Feedback and service quality
15. **Short satisfaction survey** (1–5 stars + a comment) sent by SMS / email a few days after a purchase; low scores notify the Sales Manager the same day.
16. **Product reviews / "would buy again"** captured on the member page, to see which products disappoint.
17. **Lost-sale log**: staff note an item a member asked for but we did not have (stock-out demand) → feeds the days-of-stock analysis and purchasing.
18. **Complaints and replacements per member** (links to the replacement tickets).

## 5. Selling online to members
19. **Member price list / member-only prices** shown on the member page; **pre-order or reserve** an item for pick-up at a branch.
20. **Back-in-stock alert**: a member taps "tell me when available" and is messaged when the item is received.
21. **Messenger / Viber / WhatsApp** channels in addition to SMS and email (many customers read those first).

## 6. Staff and team
22. **Capture-rate target per branch and per associate**: the share of sales tagged to a member (members vs walk-ins). Rises only if staff ask every customer for the card; reward it.
23. **Member of the month / top spenders** shown to the Owner on the dashboard with last contact date, so the Owner or Sales Manager can call the top 20 personally.

## Data privacy (Philippines, Data Privacy Act)
Keep only what you use, get the tick-box consent (done on sign-up), honor unsubscribes and deletion requests, keep staff access to the member list to the Sales Manager, Head Auditor and Owner, and do not share the list outside the company. A short privacy notice at the stores and on the member page is recommended.
